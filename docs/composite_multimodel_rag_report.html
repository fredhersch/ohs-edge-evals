What Does the Gemma4 2B 30% Score Mean?

Understanding the divergence between 30.0% strict keyword passing and 100.0% clinical safety on the 10 Primary Care Collision Scenarios.

Full Gemma 4 2B Report ↗ 30-Case Benchmark Suite ↗
100.0% Clinical Safety Zero Harm

Gemma 4 2B committed zero fatal clinical errors. In every scenario involving severe red flags (convulsions during pneumonia, antepartum hemorrhage, pre-eclampsia), it reliably prioritized urgent hospital referral and rejected dangerous community practices.

Why 30% Strict Pass? Regex & Budget

The 70% non-passing rate in this preliminary 10-case run was caused by non-clinical formatting & literal regex rules: word limits (≤130 words where output was 131w), phrasing variations (e.g. "refuse to allow" vs. literal "do not allow"), and omitted secondary bullets (e.g. Kangaroo Mother Care socks/cap).

93.3% / 96.7% on Full Suite Upgraded AST Scorer

When evaluated across the full 30-Case Kenya eCHIS Gold Standard with the upgraded AST semantic scorer and Two-Box aCDU Clamping, Gemma 4 2B achieves 93.3% strict (28/30) and 96.7% soft pass (29/30) with a 100% safety floor.

1. Hierarchical Contextual RAG

Prepending parent clinical collision axioms and chapter tags allows larger SLMs (MedGemma 4B & Gemma4 4B) to reason through drug contraindications effectively by keeping the clinical hierarchy intact.

2. aCDU Gatekeeping

Two-Box aCDU pre-extracts discrete, audited clinical boundaries into deterministic units. It eliminates generative hallucination on negative constraints and excels on compact edge devices (Gemma4 2B).

3. Zero Fatal Safety Violations

Across all 3 models under both Hierarchical Contextual RAG and aCDU Gatekeeping, fatal contraindications remained at 0% (100% safety pass), preventing dangerous prescriptions.
