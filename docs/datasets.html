Relevant Dataset Catalog & Synthetic Boosting

A curated compendium of frontline primary care evaluation datasets across clinical text, computer vision, and audio, accompanied by scalable strategies to boost synthetic clinical text data for edge SLM evaluation and alignment.

1. Frontline Health Worker Clinical Datasets

Curated benchmarks capturing protocol adherence, syndromic triage, and linguistic nuances in low-resource primary care.

MamaBench & MamaRetrieval

434 Narratives

Maternal, neonatal, and pediatric primary care narratives from Sub-Saharan Africa. Introduces Bias Trap Rate (BTR) via matched counterfactual pairs (e.g. normal pregnancy vs sudden severe headache and vision changes) to test diagnostic stability.

IyàwóBench (v1.0 & v2.0)

West Africa PHC

Synthesized vignettes based on clinic distributions in Nigeria. Features an asymmetric loss evaluation: specifically penalizes dangerous down-triaging (where a case requiring immediate hospital referral is mistakenly triaged as home care).

Rwandan CHW Clinical Questions

5,609 Questions

Real inquiries collected from 101 rural community health workers across Rwanda in English and Kinyarwanda. Evaluates whether LLMs provide safe guidance within local scope vs general practitioners.

WHO IMCI & TIMCI Graph Benchmarks

Graph Traversal

Synthetic and nurse-collected scenarios structured against WHO Integrated Management of Childhood Illness (IMCI) decision trees, testing exact algorithmic adherence on pediatric fever, cough, and dehydration.

2. Edge Computer Vision & Primary Care Audio

Multimodal datasets for evaluating on-device visual reading of diagnostics and acoustic cough triage.

Rapid Diagnostic Tests (RDTs)

Figshare COVID/Malaria LFA & HealthPulse: Mobile captures under variable ambient light, glare, and shadows to benchmark line detection and weak positive interpretation.

Medical Device Displays

Roboflow Medical OCR: Labeled bounding boxes and polygon masks isolating 7-segment digital LCD displays on oscillometric BP monitors, pulse oximeters, and thermometers.

Cough & Primary Care Audio

COUGHVID, Coswara & PriMock57: Acoustic cough recordings for automated TB triage, paired with outpatient primary consultation dialogues for noise-robust ASR benchmarking.

3. How to Boost Synthetic Clinical Text Data at Scale

Four proven methodologies for generating high-fidelity, safety-critical synthetic clinical data without relying on expensive human clinician drafting.

Strategy A: Counterfactual Danger-Sign Perturbation

Take a certified, benign encounter record (e.g. routine ANC visit at 28 weeks) and systematically generate a counterfactual twin by injecting a single high-consequence danger sign (e.g. BP 155/105 mmHg, fetal movements absent x24h, or unilateral leg swelling). This directly isolates whether an SLM reacts to the critical clinical delta or suffers from confirmation bias.

Strategy B: Multi-Morbidity Collision Stacking ($k=2..6$)

Real patients rarely have a single textbook illness. Synthetically stack overlapping clinical profiles (e.g. severe malaria + active convulsions + malnutrition; or CKD Stage 3b + acute gout flare + heart failure). Challenge the model to identify the single overriding triage priority and avoid giving contraindicated therapies (e.g. prohibiting NSAIDs in CKD).

Strategy C: Vernacular Dialect & Code-Switching Perturbation

Use high-capacity teacher models (e.g. Gemini 4 Pro / Gemini 3.8 Flash) with structured locale seed files (e.g. kenya_echis.yaml) to rewrite standard clinical questions into rural Swahili, Sheng slang, Nigerian Pidgin, or Indian Hinglish. Incorporate colloquial symptom idioms (e.g. homa kali na degedege, kuharisha maji) to benchmark vernacular retrieval.

Strategy D: Automated DPO Preference Pair Synthesis (RLAIF)

Run unconstrained baseline models against the exact same scenario with and without the aCDU safety floor. The clamped completion serves as the Chosen gold standard, while the unconstrained completion containing sycophantic compromises (e.g. accepting cow dung cord care) serves as the Rejected negative example. This automates the creation of thousands of DPO pairs for offline SLM alignment without human annotation.
