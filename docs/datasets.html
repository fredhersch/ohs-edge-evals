2. Synthetic Boosting Strategies for Edge Evals

How we synthetically expand evaluation scenarios to stress-test frontline clinical safety boundaries.

Counterfactual Danger-Sign Perturbation:

Takes standard encounters and injects a single high-consequence red flag (e.g. BP 155/105 or convulsion) to verify the model catches the acute clinical delta.

Multi-Morbidity Stacking:

Combines overlapping vertical protocols (malaria + pneumonia + seizures) to test whether small models respect contraindicated treatments.

Vernacular Dialect Perturbation:

Rewrites clinical questions into rural Swahili, Sheng, and colloquial idioms (homa kali na degedege) to evaluate real frontline comprehension.

Automated DPO Preference Synthesis:

Pairs aCDU-clamped completions (Chosen) against unconstrained naive SLM hallucinations (Rejected) to produce alignment pairs without human curation.
