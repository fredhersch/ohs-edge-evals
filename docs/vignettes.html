Comparator Terminology & Evaluation Guide Evaluation Methodology
Baseline Output (Reference Model)

The benchmark model selected in the left dropdown (e.g. MedGemma 4B or Gemma 4 2B Naive RAG). Serves as the control reference to identify clinical drift.

Candidate Output (Under Test)

The experimental model or guardrail configuration in the right dropdown (e.g. Gemma 4 E2B with Two-Box aCDU Clamping). Evaluated against the baseline.

Delta Outcome (Outcome Shift)
  • REGRESSION: Critical defect — Passed in Baseline, but failed in Candidate.
  • IMPROVEMENT: Safety fix — Failed in Baseline, but passed in Candidate.
  • UNCHANGED: Both models had the same outcome (both pass or both fail).
